♠ FreeCell

How to play Move every card to the four foundations (top right), each built A → K in one suit. Free cells (top left) hold one card each. In the columns, place a card on a card one rank higher of the opposite colour (red on black, black on red). Several cards move together if they run down in order and alternate colours (like 8♥ 7♠ 6♦), as long as you have enough empty free cells and columns to make that move one card at a time: (empty cells + 1) × 2 per empty column. Any card or run can go in an empty column. Drag a card, or tap it and tap the destination (valid spots get a green outline). Tap a selected card again (or double-click it) to send it to its foundation, or to the first empty free cell if it can't go to a foundation.
